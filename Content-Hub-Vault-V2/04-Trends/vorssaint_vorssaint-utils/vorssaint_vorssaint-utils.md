## What problem does it solve?

macOS users often need to juggle multiple small utilities (system monitoring, quick toggles, clipboard tools, etc.) that would otherwise require separate paid apps or clutter the dock. vorssaint-utils solves this by consolidating common menu bar utilities into a single free, open-source toolkit.

## How it works

It's built in Swift as a native macOS menu bar application, meaning it runs as a lightweight background process accessible from the system menu bar rather than a full app window. Being open-source and free, it likely bundles multiple discrete utility modules that users can enable/configure individually.

## Best use cases

- Quick access to system utilities (monitoring, toggles) without opening full apps
- Replacing several paid menu bar apps with one free consolidated tool
- Customizing a macOS workflow with lightweight always-available tools
