## What problem does it solve?

Typing is slow and interrupts flow, but existing voice dictation tools are either locked to specific apps, require expensive subscriptions, or produce raw unpolished transcripts full of filler words and errors. There's no universal, open-source solution that works across every app on your desktop and automatically cleans up what you say.

## How it works

You hold a configurable hotkey, speak naturally, then release — the app captures your audio, runs it through an AI transcription and polishing pipeline, and injects the cleaned text directly at your cursor position in whatever app is currently focused. Because it operates at the OS input level rather than inside a specific app, it works universally across any text field on macOS or Windows.

## Best use cases

- Drafting emails or Slack messages hands-free without leaving the app you're in
- Dictating code comments, docstrings, or commit messages directly inside your IDE
- Writing long-form content in any editor without switching to a dedicated dictation tool

## Getting started

Clone the repo from GitHub (github.com/Open-Less/openless), follow the platform-specific setup instructions for macOS or Windows, configure your hotkey and AI backend, and you're ready to dictate into any app.
