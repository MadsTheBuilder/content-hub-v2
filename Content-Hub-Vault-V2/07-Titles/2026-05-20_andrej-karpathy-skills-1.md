- "Brain distilled" is a bet. Strong Karpathy affinity = click. Casual viewer = might read as hype. The curiosity gap is real but there's no utility hook to catch someone who's on the fence.

**Title 7:** What Karpathy's Workflow Looks Like as Claude Code Skills
- Lower energy than the declarative titles above. "What X looks like as Y" barely shows up in this niche, which is either a pattern break that wins or just feels soft compared to what's actually performing. Worth testing.

- Authority-by-proxy — you're inferring his approach and the audience has to trust that. The parenthetical adds specificity but also length. If the thumbnail puts Karpathy front and center, this probably lands. Without that, it's a harder sell.

**Title 9:** The ML Developer Skill Set Nobody Teaches (Karpathy Does)

- "10M-view" is borrowed authority — verifiable, but it's meta. You're citing another channel's metrics to build credibility for your own video. Highest specificity in the set. Either signals deep research or reads as name-dropping. Strong A/B candidate against Title 1.
