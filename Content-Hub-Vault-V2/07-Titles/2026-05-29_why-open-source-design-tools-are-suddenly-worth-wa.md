Risk: Most niche of the set — leads with the "$0" outcome rather than the tool category, so it may miss search intent. Strongest with developer-practitioners who are cost-constrained; weakest with viewers who are Claude Design users curious about alternatives (they won't recognize the angle until the thumbnail connects it).
