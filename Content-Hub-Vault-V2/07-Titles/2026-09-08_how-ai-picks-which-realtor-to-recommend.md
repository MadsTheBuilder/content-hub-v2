### Tier-2 Titles (A/B Risk — Experimental)

**1. AI Recommends the Same 3 Realtors to Everyone**
Risk: leads with a claim the viewer has to trust before clicking. High curiosity gap, but no named tool and no "I tested" credibility marker — if the claim reads as speculation instead of a finding, CTR drops.

**2. Stop Optimizing Your Website. AI Doesn't Read It.**
Risk: the "Stop doing X" command pattern from #27 with an aggressive absolute claim. Polarizing — may pull strong clicks from realtors and marketers, or trigger "that's wrong" dismissal from SEO-literate viewers who bounce before the nuance lands.

**3. I Reverse-Engineered How AI Picks Realtors**
Risk: "reverse-engineered" promises deeper technical access than a black-box model allows. Strong for a developer audience, but risks an expectation mismatch if the video is empirical testing rather than actual model inspection.

**4. The Realtor Ranking Nobody Can See or Appeal**
Risk: emotional/adversarial framing with zero tool names and no clear payoff. Could break out on the unfairness angle, or fall flat as vague — it reads more like an op-ed headline than a technical video.

**5. Realtors Are Being Ranked by an Algorithm They've Never Heard Of**
Risk: longest title here, third-person and news-like rather than first-person testing. Wider potential reach beyond the dev audience, but no "I built/tested" authority signal, which is the strongest pattern in the competitor set.
