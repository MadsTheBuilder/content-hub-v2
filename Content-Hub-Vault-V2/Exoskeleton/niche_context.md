# Niche Context — AI Tools & Build-in-Public

## Your Niche
AI updates, new tool releases, use-case breakdowns (what tool excels where, is it worth it or just hype), and your personal journey building tools like Content Hub.

## Content Pillars
1. **AI Tool Reviews** — New releases, feature breakdowns, honest "worth it vs hype" verdicts
2. **AI Use Cases** — Practical workflows: "I used X to do Y, here's what happened"
3. **Build in Public** — Your journey building Content Hub and other tools, lessons learned, behind-the-scenes
4. **Industry Commentary** — AI industry trends, what's coming, who's winning/losing

## Target Audience
- Developers and tech-curious professionals (25-45)
- People who want to USE AI tools, not just hear about them
- Aspiring builders who want to see how someone actually ships products with AI
- Business owners curious about AI automation

## Your Unique Angle
You're not a reporter reading press releases — you're a **builder who uses these tools daily**. When you review an AI tool, you've actually built something with it. When you talk about AI trends, you've got skin in the game. This is what separates you from generic "Top 10 AI Tools" channels.

## Platform Focus
- **YouTube** — Primary. Daily videos (5-15 min). Mix of tool reviews, tutorials, build vlogs.
- **LinkedIn** — Professional distribution. AI industry takes, build-in-public updates.
- **Twitter/X** — Quick takes, thread breakdowns of longer videos, tool announcements.
- **TikTok/Reels** — Short-form cuts from long videos (30-60s highlights).

## Tone & Style
- Conversational but knowledgeable — "I tried this so you don't have to"
- Honest, sometimes contrarian — willing to say "this is overhyped"
- Builder's perspective — show the actual workflow, not just the marketing page
- Energy: Medium-high, enthusiastic about good tech, skeptical about bad marketing
