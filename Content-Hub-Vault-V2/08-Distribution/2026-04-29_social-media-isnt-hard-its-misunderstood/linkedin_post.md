Still struggling to grow your audience and share your work in AI or dev productivity? The fundamental rules of social media aren't what they used to be, and most advice hasn't caught up.

Here's a breakdown of how the game actually works now:

*   **The Shift:** Platforms like LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok aren't optimizing for "social" connections anymore. They've shifted to pure "media" consumption, prioritizing attention above all else.
*   **Algorithm's Core Mission:** The algorithm's job is singular: *audience matching*. It wants to connect *any* relevant content with viewers who will stay engaged, regardless of who they follow. Think of it as a sophisticated recommendation engine.
*   **Your Advantage:** As a creator, your objective is to help the platform make these optimal matches. When your content successfully keeps viewers watching or interacting, the algorithm rewards you with significantly wider distribution.
*   **Actionable Strategy:** This means letting go of old "social" growth hacks. Instead, focus relentlessly on creating high-value, specific media that genuinely resonates with a clear audience, whether it's about a new AI tool, a developer productivity hack, or a build-in-public update.
