# LinkedIn Post

Andrej Karpathy keeps shipping 300-line repos that teach more than most ML courses.

Here's what his project list actually reveals about learning AI in 2026.

→ **micrograd** is ~150 lines of Python. It builds a working autograd engine from scratch — enough to train a binary classifier. If backprop still feels like magic, start here, not with PyTorch tutorials.

→ **nanoGPT** is the rewrite of minGPT optimized for speed without losing readability. It's the fastest path from "I've heard of transformers" to actually training one on your own machine.

→ **llm.c** pretrains GPT-2 in pure C/CUDA — no PyTorch, no frameworks. Slightly faster than PyTorch Nightly. The point isn't speed though. It's seeing what the abstractions hide.

→ **llama2.c** is a full inference stack in a single 700-line C file. He called it a weekend project. Most teams can't ship that in a quarter.

→ The pattern: strip the framework, hardcode the architecture, keep it under a few hundred lines. Education through subtraction.

His "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" YouTube series walks through building each of these live. It's the closest thing to an apprenticeship in deep learning that exists for free.
