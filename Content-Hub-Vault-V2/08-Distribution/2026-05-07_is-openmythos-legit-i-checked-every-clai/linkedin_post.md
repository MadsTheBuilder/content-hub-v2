The OpenMythos project claims a 770M parameter model can perform like a 1.3B model by rethinking architecture.
But digging into the details reveals a more nuanced story than a simple benchmark comparison.

*   **Recurrent Depth, Not More Layers:** The core idea is "thinking deeper" by iteratively looping through a small set of layers, aiming for parameter efficiency over traditional scaling.
*   **Silent Reasoning in Latent Space:** OpenMythos proposes performing complex reasoning internally within hidden states, which could reduce token costs and increase inference speed compared to explicit chain-of-thought methods.
*   **Performance Claims Under Scrutiny:** While promising, critics note the project currently lacks trained weights, verifiable benchmarks, and faces questions about its code's stability and the origin of its performance metrics.
*   **A Research Sandbox, Not a Production Stack:** Consider OpenMythos a credible *architectural experiment* and a valuable research artifact for exploring compute and parameter efficiency, rather than a finished, production-ready model.
