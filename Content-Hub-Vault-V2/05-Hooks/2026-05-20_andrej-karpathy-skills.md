# Hook Variations: Andrej Karpathy skills

### Hook 1 — Contrast-Driven Curiosity (The Curiosity Loop)

**Spoken Line** (first 3 seconds — stop the scroll):
> Karpathy just killed prompt engineering.

**Setup** (next 10 seconds — deepen the curiosity gap):
> For two years, every AI tutorial taught you the same thing. Write better prompts. Chain them. Evaluate them. Karpathy spent 9 minutes showing why that whole era is already over — and most engineers scrolled past it.

**Promise** (next 15 seconds — what they get if they keep watching):
> I'll show you the three skills he's betting the next decade of AI engineering on, why prompting is the wrong mental model now, and the file structure he's using to replace it — the one frontier labs are already shipping.

**Visual Cue** (what is on screen during the hook):
> Karpathy's face frozen mid-sentence on the left. On the right, the word "PROMPT" with a red strike-through glitches into "SKILL."

**Text Overlay:**
> Karpathy: "Prompting is dead."

**Kallaway Pattern:** Contrast-Driven Curiosity (The Curiosity Loop)
**Why:** Pits the viewer's existing mental model (better prompts = better AI) against a contrarian claim from the most credible voice in the field, and refuses to resolve it.

### Hook 2 — The 6-Power-Word Stack

**Spoken Line** (first 3 seconds — stop the scroll):
> If I had to learn AI engineering from zero in 2026, I'd copy Karpathy's 4 skills.

**Setup** (next 10 seconds — deepen the curiosity gap):
> Not his Stanford lectures. Not Tesla. Not nanoGPT. The four unsexy capabilities he keeps pointing to in every recent talk — the ones that separate engineers who ship AI products from engineers who just talk about them.

**Promise** (next 15 seconds — what they get if they keep watching):
> I spent two weeks reverse-engineering every Karpathy talk, podcast, and commit from the last 12 months. You'll get the full list, the order to learn them in, and the one he says 95% of engineers skip — which is exactly why they stall.

**Visual Cue** (what is on screen during the hook):
> Split screen. Left: Karpathy at a whiteboard. Right: a numbered list "1, 2, 3, 4" with the items blurred, animating into focus one by one.

**Text Overlay:**
> The 4 skills · Ranked · From scratch

**Kallaway Pattern:** The 6-Power-Word Stack
**Why:** Stacks subject ("AI engineering"), action ("learn"), end-state ("from zero"), contrast ("copy Karpathy's"), proof ("4 skills"), and time ("in 2026") — the canonical credibility-plus-urgency frame.

### Hook 3 — Pain Point Agitation (Expected Value) ⭐

**Spoken Line** (first 3 seconds — stop the scroll):

**Setup** (next 10 seconds — deepen the curiosity gap):
> It's not the papers you didn't read. It's not the course you skipped. Karpathy named the gap in a recent talk and almost nobody picked up on it, because it sounds boring. It isn't. It's the reason your AI side projects keep stalling at 80% done.

**Promise** (next 15 seconds — what they get if they keep watching):
> In 8 minutes I'll walk you through the four skills Karpathy says actually matter in 2026, the order he learned them, and a weekend project for each — so by Monday you've closed the gap most engineers will spend another year ignoring.

**Visual Cue** (what is on screen during the hook):
> Code editor showing a half-finished AI agent script with a blinking cursor. Karpathy's photo fades in on the right with a quote underline animating.

**Text Overlay:**
> Senior engineers · Still behind on AI · Here's why

**Kallaway Pattern:** Pain Point Agitation (Expected Value)
**Why:** Names a specific, identity-anchored pain (senior + still behind) and promises an immediately actionable fix — the highest-converting shape for technical viewers who already feel the problem.

**Visual Cue** (what is on screen during the hook):
> Bold red "90%" smashes onto a black background, then dissolves into Karpathy's face mid-talk with a subtle zoom-in.

**Text Overlay:**

**Kallaway Pattern:** Shock Value (The Awareness Play)
