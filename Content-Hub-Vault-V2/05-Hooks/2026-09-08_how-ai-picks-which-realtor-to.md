# Hook Variations: How AI picks which realtor to recommend

### Hook 1 — The 6-Power-Word Stack

**Spoken Line** (first 3 seconds — stop the scroll):
> "I asked AI for the best realtor in my city 40 times. It named the same three people every time."

**Setup** (next 10 seconds — deepen the curiosity gap):
> "Twelve thousand licensed agents here. Three of them own the answer. And none of the three are top producers."

**Promise** (next 15 seconds — what they get if they keep watching):
> "In six minutes I'll show you what the model is actually scoring when it picks a name. Not reviews. Not sales volume. Once you see it, you'll know why AI recommends some products and never mentions yours."

**Visual Cue** (what is on screen during the hook):
> Hard cut into a screen recording: 40 AI answers scrolling fast in a grid, three names snapping to bright orange as they repeat. Whip zoom to those three names alone on black.

**Text Overlay:**
> 40 PROMPTS → 3 NAMES

**Kallaway Pattern:** The 6-Power-Word Stack
**Why:** Hard proof numbers ("40 times," "same three") make the claim testable, so the brain files it as evidence and stays for the mechanism.

### Hook 2 — Contrast-Driven Curiosity (The Curiosity Loop)

**Spoken Line** (first 3 seconds — stop the scroll):
> "AI isn't picking the best realtor. It's picking the most describable one."

**Setup** (next 10 seconds — deepen the curiosity gap):
> "We think these systems rank quality. They can't. Quality isn't in the training data. Something else is — and that something decides who gets named and who stays invisible."

**Promise** (next 15 seconds — what they get if they keep watching):
> "Realtors are just the cleanest test case. No leaderboard to hide behind. What you're about to see runs under every recommendation AI makes — including the ones about your tool and your work."

**Visual Cue** (what is on screen during the hook):
> Split screen: left, a dense grid of agent sales numbers draining to grey. Right, one glowing paragraph of plain-text bio. The grey half drops to near-black on the word "describable."

**Text Overlay:**
> BEST ≠ RECOMMENDED

**Kallaway Pattern:** Contrast-Driven Curiosity (The Curiosity Loop)
**Why:** It kills the viewer's default belief in one sentence, then refuses to name the replacement — the loop stays open.

### Hook 3 — The You/Your Pivot ⭐

**Spoken Line** (first 3 seconds — stop the scroll):
> "If you want AI to recommend what you built, you need the realtor problem."

**Setup** (next 10 seconds — deepen the curiosity gap):
> "Twelve thousand agents. No public ranking. No official best. The model picks one anyway, confidently, every time. That same call is being made about your product right now, in someone else's chat window."

**Promise** (next 15 seconds — what they get if they keep watching):
> "I traced the full path — retrieval, source weighting, and the one thing that gets a name spoken out loud. By the end you'll know which of those you can move, and which one is why you're getting skipped."

**Visual Cue** (what is on screen during the hook):
> Open tight on a chat window mid-generation, cursor blinking. Whip-pan to a node graph of thousands of grey dots. One ignites orange. Hold.

**Text Overlay:**
> WHY IT PICKS THEM, NOT YOU

**Kallaway Pattern:** The You/Your Pivot (Relevance Strategy)
**Why:** It converts a niche topic into the builder's own stakes in the first sentence, so the "not about me" drop-off never fires.

### Hook 4 — Shock Value (The Awareness Play)

**Spoken Line** (first 3 seconds — stop the scroll):
> "Every AI recommendation you've ever gotten was a popularity contest you never saw."

**Setup** (next 10 seconds — deepen the curiosity gap):
> "Not a ranking. Not a review score. Decided by who got written about in a shape the model could reuse. I proved it on the messiest market I could find — realtors — where quality can't be measured and AI answers anyway."

**Promise** (next 15 seconds — what they get if they keep watching):
> "I'll run the test on camera, show you the raw outputs, and name the signal doing the work. Then I'll show you the same pattern in a category you actually care about."

**Visual Cue** (what is on screen during the hook):
> Full-frame white type on hot orange, one word per beat, cutting on the audio hits. Smash cut to face-cam, extreme reaction, then straight into the raw output feed.

**Text Overlay:**
> IT'S NOT A RANKING

**Kallaway Pattern:** Shock Value (The Awareness Play)
**Why:** A counter-intuitive premise aimed at everyone who has ever asked an AI for a recommendation pushes reach far past the niche.

---

### Hook 5 — Anticipation / The Edging Hook

**Spoken Line** (first 3 seconds — stop the scroll):
> "I found the signal that decides which realtor AI recommends. Then I checked it. I was wrong."

**Setup** (next 10 seconds — deepen the curiosity gap):
> "My first guess was reviews. Most reviews, most trust, most recommendations. So I pulled review counts for every name that came up. The top-recommended agent wasn't in the top fifty."

**Promise** (next 15 seconds — what they get if they keep watching):
> "It took three wrong theories to find the real one. I'll walk you through all three, because the wrong ones explain why the right one works — and why it's the same mechanism deciding whether your work ever gets named."

**Visual Cue** (what is on screen during the hook):
> Orange checkmark slams onto a bar chart labeled REVIEWS. Red X wipes across it on "wrong." Chart shatters into a whiteboard of three theories, two already crossed out.

**Text Overlay:**
> THEORY 1 OF 3 ❌

**Kallaway Pattern:** Anticipation / The Edging Hook
**Why:** It hands over the obvious answer and yanks it back in the same breath, resetting the loop before the brain can close it.
