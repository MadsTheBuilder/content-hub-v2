**Promise** (next 15 seconds — what they get if they keep watching):

**Visual Cue** (what is on screen during the hook):
> Hard cut from a black screen to terminal: `git clone andrej-karpathy-skills` typing itself. Karpathy's face frozen on the right side, then dissolves into a folder of `.md` files. Bright cyan-on-black.

**Text Overlay:**
> KARPATHY → INSTALLED

**Kallaway Pattern:** The 6-Power-Word Stack
**Why:** Pairs a famous name (Subject Clarity) with a concrete action (open-sourced/install), an end-state (his brain as skills), and a time constraint (60 seconds) — credibility plus urgency in one breath.
