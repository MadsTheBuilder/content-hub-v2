# Hook Variations: Why open-source design tools are suddenly worth watching

### Hook 1 — The You/Your Pivot

**Spoken Line** (first 3 seconds — stop the scroll):
> "If you're building with a coding agent and still paying for a design tool, you're wasting money."

**Setup** (next 10 seconds — deepen the curiosity gap):
> "An open-source design system plugs directly into Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor. It detects your agent, uses it as the engine. No separate AI. No extra subscription."

**Promise** (next 15 seconds — what they get if they keep watching):
> "In 7 minutes I'll show you how it works, why it beats anything you're paying for, and the one setup step that unlocks 71 production-ready design systems — free."

**Visual Cue** (what is on screen during the hook):
> Creator pointing at a side-by-side: left — a design tool invoice; right — the open-source tool generating a full UI prototype in real time.

**Text Overlay:**
> "You're paying for this → This does it free"

**Kallaway Pattern:** The You/Your Pivot (Relevance Strategy)
**Why:** Frames the AI builder as the subject in the first six words — the "does this apply to me?" filter is dead before the second sentence.
