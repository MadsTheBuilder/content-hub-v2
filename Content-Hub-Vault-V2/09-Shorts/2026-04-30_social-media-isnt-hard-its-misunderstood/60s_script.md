**SPOKEN SCRIPT:**
Your content strategy is wrong. Social media isn't social anymore. It's just media. This is why your growth is stuck. Old strategies focused on followers and connections. But since 2020, platforms like Instagram and YouTube shifted. TikTok proved that media consumption, not social connection, keeps users engaged. Now, every platform's algorithm prioritizes keeping viewers on the app to watch more ads. They don't care if you have a million followers or zero. Their only job is audience matching. If your video keeps a stranger watching, the platform wins. And if the platform wins, you win. It will push your content to more ideal viewers. Your job as a creator is simple: help the algorithm match your content with the right viewer. Focus on creating media that hooks and holds the attention of strangers. That's how you grow fast.
