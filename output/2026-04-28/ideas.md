## 🎯 Core Content Strategy: The "Brutal Expert" Angle

Your niche should be framed not as "AI tutorials," but as **"The AI Workflow Engineer."**

*   **Authority:** You don't just show *how* to use a tool; you show *why* a tool is bad, *how* to combine tools for 10x results, and *where* the market is going in 6 months.
*   **Tone:** Highly knowledgeable, slightly aggressive, and extremely helpful. You are teaching people how to skip the learning curve and get straight to the money/result.
*   **Goal:** To build a reputation as the person who makes people feel dumb if they *don't* watch the video.

## 🚀 5 Viral Content Pillars (Video Ideas)

These ideas leverage trending psychological hooks (scarcity, controversy, massive productivity gains) while remaining deeply technical.

### 💡 Pillar 1: The "Controversial/Debunking" Video
**(Topic: AI Image Generation)**

*   **Title Idea:** "Stop Using Midjourney: Why This Free AI is Better (And Why They Don't Want You To Know)"
*   **Hook:** Start by admitting the major players (Midjourney, DALL-E) are overrated for professionals.
*   **Execution:** Dedicate 80% of the video to showing a specific, lesser-known tool (e.g., local Stable Diffusion setups, or a specific Adobe plugin) that solves a niche, high-value problem better than the big players.
*   **Viral Angle:** Controversy and fear of missing out (FOMO) on "the secret."

### 💡 Pillar 2: The "Extreme Productivity Hack" Video
**(Topic: Workflow Engineering)**

*   **Title Idea:** "My $0 AI Workflow That Replaces 4 Employees (The Complete System)"
*   **Hook:** Show the final, amazing result first (e.g., a fully generated, researched, and published article or marketing campaign) and then say, "Here is how I did it."
*   **Execution:** This video must be a *system demonstration*, not just a sequence of commands. Show how Tool A outputs data that is perfectly formatted for Tool B, which then populates a Notion/Airtable dashboard.
*   **Viral Angle:** Promise of massive time/money return. This speaks directly to the "busy professional" demographic.

### 💡 Pillar 3: The "Educational Deep Dive" Video
**(Topic: LLM Prompt Engineering Theory)**

*   **Title Idea:** "The 5 Prompt Engineering Concepts You Aren't Learning in College"
*   **Hook:** Promise to teach the *theory* behind the prompt, not just the prompt itself.
*   **Execution:** Teach concepts like **Role-Playing Depth, Chain-of-Thought (CoT),** and **Self-Correction Loops** by demonstrating them in action. Make it look academic but keep the pace snappy.
*   **Viral Angle:** Authority and intellectual curiosity. This establishes you as a genuine expert, not just a tutorial-maker.

### 💡 Pillar 4: The "Future Prediction/Warning" Video
**(Topic: Ethical AI / Job Disruption)**

*   **Title Idea:** "The Next 12 Months: Which Job Skill AI Can *Never* Replicate (And How to Learn It)"
*   **Hook:** Start with a stark warning: "If your job involves [X, Y, Z task], you are already obsolete."
*   **Execution:** Frame AI as a **copilot,** not a replacement. Focus on the *human* skills that become premium: high-level strategic thinking, cross-industry synthesis, and emotionally intelligent communication.
*   **Viral Angle:** Fear of obsolescence (a highly potent motivator).

### 💡 Pillar 5: The "Resource Roundup/Mini-Guide" Video
**(Topic: AI for Specific Niches)**

*   **Title Idea:** "Top 5 AI Tools for Indie Game Developers (And the One You Should Avoid)"
*   **Hook:** Call out a specific, highly valuable niche audience.
*   **Execution:** Keep it fast. Spend 90 seconds on 5 tools. The "Avoid" section adds credibility by showing you aren't just shilling every product.
*   **Viral Angle:** Hyper-relevance. People search for solutions to *their* specific problems.
